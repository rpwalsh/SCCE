# Finish the platform

SCCE works end to end. This is not a rewrite. Preserve demonstrated behaviour and make the architecture honest,
selective, measurable and complete.

**Never perform cognition because an operator exists. Perform it because the unresolved proof obligation justifies
its cost.** Global compressed structure, then local proof pressure, then selective computation, then exact
evidence, then proof.

**Do not invent more architecture unless measurement proves it is required.** There is enough architecture. The
work is making it observable, selective, causally validated and complete.

## Six claims that are not the same claim

implemented · wired · configured · executed · causal · useful

Report them separately. A component that returns identity because its learned artifact is missing is
`inert_unconfigured`, never a successful run. Output written only to diagnostics is diagnostic, not cognition.
Approximation may select where to look; only exact evidence may certify a claim.

## Five phases, each with a hard acceptance

1. **Observable.** Every subsystem has an explicit state and a measured cost. Acceptance: an external program can
   read one production trace and answer what ran, what did not, what was inert, what each cost, and what changed
   causal state. No operator hidden inside a diagnostic blob.
2. **Remove eager waste.** Cheapest precondition predicting "this cannot improve the proof", then replay.
   Acceptance: same or stronger proof outcomes, zero new unsupported claims, measured reduction in wall and CPU.
   Faster with weaker proof semantics is a FAIL.
3. **Proof-directed scheduler.** Proof frontier, operation bids, explicit stop rule. No neural policy; empirical
   tables are enough. Acceptance: easy turns skip expensive cognition, hard turns buy it, and every invocation is
   explainable by a recorded decision.
4. **Artifact lifecycles.** fit, hold out, validate, promote, persist, select, hydrate, trace the model id.
   Acceptance: no diagram may call a learned component active unless a production trace carries the exact promoted
   artifact identity that participated. Hardcoding a model into config does not count.
5. **Ingestion starvation.** Measure the funnel before touching a threshold. Acceptance: more answer-grade
   structure on a held-out corpus with no increase in unsupported relation admission. Relation count alone is not
   the metric.

## Finished means

An inert subsystem cannot look active. Every cognitive operation is observable and attributable. Expensive eager
work has evidence for why it runs. Easy turns avoid cognition they do not need and hard turns can buy more.
Learned components complete their lifecycle. Ingestion yields enough structure to reason over. Existing answers are
preserved or better. At least one sophisticated operator has a measured causal contribution on a benchmark that
requires it. And for any answer the system can say what evidence it used, what obligations it resolved, what ran,
what it cost, which operations changed the result, and why it stopped.

The statement to make true, not to optimize: **SCCE does not globally activate an intelligence model. It maintains
persistent structured knowledge and selectively purchases computation only where unresolved proof requires it.**

## Swarm shape

Runtime archaeologist, operator economics, ingestion funnel, synthesizer, implementer, skeptic. Investigators do
not modify production; they produce measurements and falsifiable conclusions. The synthesizer picks the smallest
intervention the evidence supports. The skeptic tries to prove the speedup is fake, the proof quality weakened, the
attribution circular, the telemetry lying, or that hidden eager work remains.

Negative results are deliverables. Do not tune until a diagnostic set passes.
