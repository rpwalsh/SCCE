## Mechanism built (frozen over the whole vocabulary before any pair was scored)

- **1. Canonical edit families.** Minimum edit script aligned from both ends: longest common prefix `p`, longest
  common suffix `s`, transformation `(leftContext "*", from, to, rightContext = the shared suffix)`, stem = the
  shared prefix. `find/found`, `bind/bound`, `grind/ground`, `wind/wound` all reduce to `i>ou@nd` as required.
  Admission rule (an information condition, not a number): `p + s >= 1` and `|from| + |to| <= p + s` — the
  invariant material must be at least the alternating material, or there is no context and the "rule" is just the
  two strings. Enumerated by prefix buckets (stem at least half the word, which is what the information condition
  permits) and by first/last-character buckets; buckets over 4000 skipped as a cost bound.
- **2. Population against a null.** Null = an order-3 interpolated absolute-discounting **character** model over
  the type inventory, discount `D = n1/(n1+2n2) = 0.5781` from the corpus itself. `P(string in V) = |V| * 2^-bits`.
  `logBF` = log likelihood ratio of one fitted membership rate against that per-stem null, BIC-penalised.
- **3. MDL.** `independentBits = sum over members of bits(stem+to+rc)`;
  `modelBits = bits(from) + bits(to) + bits(rc) + |eligible| * H2(p̂) + 0.5 log2|eligible|`
  (the transformation, the optimal membership bitmap over the eligible population — exceptions are its zeros — and
  the code for the rate). Accept when `gainBits > 0` and `logBF > 0`.
- **4. Role-conditioned correspondence.** Per-lexeme-normalised pooled left-context profiles for the from-side and
  the to-side of the family (normalising per lexeme is the role abstraction: shape, not mass), smoothed by a
  Dirichlet with the corpus background as base measure and one pseudo-observation. Statistic
  `role = (ownFrom - crossFrom) + (ownTo - crossTo)`, leave-one-out.
- T6's free-form lexicon is **reproduced exactly** inside the harness (same fit `log k = -0.129 + 0.832 log t`,
  sd 0.421, 17,364 forms; same Mills bound) and composed as a precondition. `free-form-lexicon.ts` untouched.

Corpus-wide, on the resident models: **33,926 transformations induced, 1,771 accepted by MDL+null, 1,522 of them
with both sides non-empty** (true alternations, not affixation). Largest accepted family 2,284 members.

## Ablations — the family hypothesis is NOT supported

Three conditions were run as instructed. **SHUFFLED FAMILY does not destroy the contextual signal**, so the
correspondence statistic is not family-driven:

| pair | FULL role | SHUFFLED role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| find/found | 2.324 | 2.141 |
| capital/capitals | 1.489 | 0.266 |
| held-out `s>ed@` acts/acted | 4.341 | **5.583** |
| held-out `s>ed@` films/filmed | 7.085 | **8.062** |
| held-out `s>ed@` works/worked | 4.263 | **6.125** |

On the held-out family (`s>ed@`, 298 members of 3,815 eligible, logBF 737.5, gain 4,986.6 bits — chosen by rank,
never inspected while the mechanism was written) the shuffled pool scores **higher** than the true pool on 6 of 12
members. Verdict: **this is a more elaborate pairwise classifier with a family-shaped wrapper, not family evidence.**
Reported as instructed rather than shipped.

One honest caveat on the shuffle: non-member stems have no to-side surface at all, so the shuffled to-side pool
collapses toward the true members. The control is therefore weaker than intended — but it is weaker in the
direction that would *help* the hypothesis, and the hypothesis still fails.

NO FAMILY (pair evidence only) is decisively negative everywhere: `gainBits` is -11 to -23 bits for every pair,
i.e. a one-member transformation never pays for itself. That part behaves correctly; it just does not discriminate.

## Two mechanism failures worth carrying forward

- **`discover/discovery` is killed by the role statistic** (role -0.325). The `0>y@` transformation is
  overwhelmingly accepted (165 members, logBF 228, 1,794 bits) but the contextual correspondence refuses this
  member. Any future context factor must not reproduce this.
- **`bind/bound` is killed by T6's composition**: `bind` has `t=5` in the resident models, so its verdict is
  *unknown*, and requiring both sides *free* refuses the pair. Composing T6 as a hard precondition is therefore
  wrong at low frequency; it belongs in the product as a likelihood factor, which is what the revised brief says.
