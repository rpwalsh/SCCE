## The defect, in one sentence

`answerCoversRequest` (`packages/kernel/src/local-evidence-runtime.ts`) requires every relation unit of a request
to be carried by the answering sentence, and derives that relation by subtracting the request's named anchors
from its content units -- but those anchors are the request's maximal *content runs* whenever the corpus attests
no identity for them, so `"Albert Einstein's dentist"` is one run, the asked attribute lands inside the subject,
the subtraction returns an empty obligation, and **an empty obligation was treated as a satisfied one**.

## How it was found (not guessed)

Three abstention rows traced live through `.scce/traces`:

- `mouth.deterministic.select` reports the Einstein lead with `covers: true`. The gate ran and passed it; nothing
  bypassed it.
- `local_evidence.coverage` reports `contentUnits: ["dentist"]` with `content: 0` on all six top-ranked sentences.
- `local_evidence.plan.rank` reports `anchors: ["albert einstein's", "albert", "albert einstein's dentist",
  "einstein's dentist"]` -- the anchor carrying the attribute is what empties the subtraction.
- `candidate.realization_contract` reports `contractSource: "none", requiredRelationUnits: []` on all three turns.

A second seam, found the same way: "What was Albert Einstein's shoe size?" had every deterministic surface
rejected and `mouth.generate` reporting `candidate:deterministic:continuation-required` -- the mouth spoke
nothing -- and then `mouth.source_summary_fallback` emitted four sentences of the biography anyway. That lane had
no answerhood check at all; it was gated only on `evidenceIdentityBindsRequest`, which asks whether the request
names the source. Pure resemblance. L6 measured it at 34 of the 44 rows.

## The fix, at the seam

From `proof-addressed-memory.md`: score discrimination, not resemblance. A sentence carrying nothing of the asked
relation supports every candidate value for that relation equally -- `D(e) = 0` -- so it settles nothing.

Four commits:

- **`fdedd83`** -- when the subtraction empties, re-derive the obligation. New primitive
  `requestRelationBeyondSourceIdentity(requestText, span, languageClosedClass)`: the request's content units that
  the **source's own title and derived identity** do not account for. Empty exactly when the request asks about
  the subject itself, which the source's own definition answers. Applied at three sites that shared the vacuity:
  the gate, `mouth.contradiction_fallback`'s `relatesBeyondSubject`, and `mouth.source_summary_fallback` (new
  `evidenceDiscriminatesAskedRelation` check).
- **`e084da2`** -- make it structural. The first version only re-derived for a caller holding the language's
  learned closed class, which left the hole open wherever runtime corpus identity is absent or stale -- and L2
  showed it can be a full turn stale. Now when the subtraction empties the SUBJECT comes from the source's title
  and identity, needing no runtime signal, and that same subject is what the answering sentence must name. This
  closed the other half of the vacuum: an anchor that swallowed the whole request was satisfied by the relation
  word itself, so "Their son Eduard was born in Zurich" counted as naming Einstein because "born" was inside the
  anchor.
- **`47cea31`** -- the subject cannot be the thing that proves the relation was answered. The subtraction read the
  subject by string identity while every other comparison in the gate reads by shared stem, so an inflected
  subject stayed in the obligation: `{einstein's, dentist}`, the lead satisfied "einstein's" by saying
  "Einstein", and one missing unit out of two is exactly the shape `categoryMemberAnswer` forgives. Also strips
  the request's opening scaffolding and the language's closed class from the source-relative remainder, because
  an obligation names what the ANSWER must carry: "When did the American Revolutionary War end?" reached the gate
  as `{what, year, american, revolutionary}` and would otherwise have demanded the answer restate "what".
- **`9b3c0a3`** -- bulletin.

No new constant. No casing, suffix, word-list or position rule: the split is title-versus-request, unit matching
is the existing corpus-derived `requestUnitSharesStem`, and the scaffolding set is the language's own learned
closed class (`deriveClosedClassWords`, Kneser-Ney continuation diversity) threaded from callers that already
hold it.

Regression check `packages/kernel/src/__tests__/answerhood-discrimination.test.ts`, 8 assertions, each verified to
fail with its own fix neutralised and pass with it.

## Two things I was asked to fix and did not

**`categoryMemberAnswer` is one unit short across a family, and relaxing the count re-opens abstention.** L6 is
right about the mechanism: on `athens-country` the units are `["athens","capital","which","country"]`, no answer
can restate "which" or "country", so `missingRelationUnits` is 2 and the escape never fires. But relaxing
`missingRelationUnits.length === 1` to "the last content unit is among the missing" is not safe, and the
counterexample is in this workload: `reference:aberdeen-absent-mascot` asks for "the official city mascot of
Aberdeen", the emitted sentence carries "city", the missing units are `{official, mascot}` with "mascot" last,
and the sentence names an entity outside the request -- so the relaxed escape fires and the row speaks again.
Request structure alone does not separate "the category the answer instantiates" (country, dynasty, kingdom) from
"the attribute the corpus lacks" (mascot, dinosaur, blood type); both are trailing units the answer would
replace. Separating them needs the relation graph -- does the entity the sentence names actually stand in the
asked relation to the subject -- not this gate. I did what is safe instead: the escape now cannot fire on a
one-unit obligation, which is what removed the Einstein-dentist route.

**The family is currently unreachable anyway, and that is the more urgent finding.** On `L2-factual.json`, every
row of that family admits *zero evidence*: `durrani-founder`, `ashoka-dynasty`, `alp-country`, `ainu-country` and
`athens-country` all went from evidence 2 to evidence 0, with empty answers. Fourteen factual rows are at
evidence 0 where the baseline had four. An answerhood gate cannot zero the evidence field -- it runs on admitted
evidence, and the baseline proves declining does not clear the field (`apollo8-absent-camera` and
`anglicanism-absent-founder2099` both declined *with* evidence 2). Those ten rows are a retrieval regression
upstream of every gate, and no repair to `categoryMemberAnswer` can be validated while they admit nothing.

## What was proved false

- **"The gate is being bypassed."** It is not. `mouth.deterministic.select` records `covers: true` for the lead.
- **"The semantic realization contract would have caught this."** It never binds on these turns:
  `contractSource: "none"`, `requiredRelationUnits: []` on all three traced rows.
- **"The relation words were just missing from the request's units."** For the 1998 World Cup they were: the
  mouth's coverage units were `{fifa, world}` because `requestClosedClassWords` folds in the interaction corpus's
  construction literals and had eaten "1998". That is why the re-derivation reads the *language's* closed class
  and re-reads the request rather than trusting the caller's filtered list.
- **"My re-derivation endangers cloze."** It cannot fire on a cloze prompt: it is gated on the subject
  subtraction coming back empty, and a cloze prompt has dozens of content units against a handful of corpus
  identities. Cloze measured 125/160 against 112, up 13.
- **A bare year never reaches `requestRelationBeyondSourceIdentity`.** `requestContentAnchorUnits` drops any unit
  with at most one letter as a generic question signal, so "1998" is gone before the obligation is built. One of
  my own assertions encoded the opposite; it is corrected with the reason recorded rather than deleted. What
  refuses the tournament's definition is the numeric-qualifier rule that already tells Apollo from Apollo 11 --
  and `q-none-cloze-002` shows it does not always: that row still speaks.
- **Suite defect, not an engine defect:** `reference:adelaide-absent-mayor` was marked unanswerable while the
  corpus states it ("the current being Lord Mayor 'The Right Honourable' Jane Lomax-Smith"). Confirmed by the
  coordinator and regraded answerable; SCCE answers it.
