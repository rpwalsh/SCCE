## The measured property that separates body from apparatus

Two measurements, both of the source's own typography. Neither is a keyword list, a title-word list, a character
offset, a casing rule or a hand-set number.

### 1. Is a line break in this source a sentence end, or the printer's wrap?

Compare how often a line that ENDS a paragraph closes a sentence against how often a line INSIDE a paragraph does.
Both rates come from the text. If interior lines close less often, interior breaks carry no sentence information and
are joined.

| source | paragraph-final lines closing | interior lines closing | decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| moby dick | 2513 @ 97.5% | 16711 @ 4.9% | joined as wrap |
| pride and prejudice | 2314 @ 81.0% | 9258 @ 5.4% | joined as wrap |
| albania (wiki, non-fiction) | 1 @ 100% | 0 | **kept** -- text untouched |

Effect on where apparatus ranks (ground truth labelled by hand only for the measurement: body = "Call me Ishmael" ..
"END OF THE PROJECT", "It is a truth universally" .. end):

| source | apparatus in top 10 / rank of the first apparatus sentence | after joining the wrap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| moby dick | 5 / rank 5 | 0 / rank 188 |
| pride and prejudice | 1 / rank 6 | 0 / rank 82 |
| jane eyre | 0 / rank 72 | 0 / rank 144 |
| dracula | 0 / rank 289 | 0 / rank 314 |

### 2. Did the source terminate this segment?

A title page, a running head, a table-of-contents line, a publisher's imprint, a wiki section heading and a
`*** START OF THE PROJECT GUTENBERG EBOOK ***` banner are segments the source never closed. Where a source
demonstrably terminates most of its segments, one it did not terminate is not one of its sentences. Terminators are
the existing cross-script `SENTENCE_BOUNDARY_SYMBOLS`. Dropped counts: 19/551 (Moby Dick identity window), 679/7774
(Pride and Prejudice), 52/856 (albania).

### 3. Similarity: shared information, not the cosine

The cosine measures agreement of direction, so a two-word label is a unit vector on one rare unit and scores ~1.0
against every other label. Measured on the Moby Dick identity window, the top of the ranking was:

```
rank  mass   deg  maxSim  sentence
    1   5.02   13 1.000   ENGLISH SAILOR.
    3   4.93   12 1.000   SPANISH SAILOR.
    5   4.28   13 1.000   OLD MANX SAILOR.
   17   3.10   12 0.519   Title: Moby Dick; Or, The Whale
```

Replaced with TextRank's normalization over LexRank's weights -- shared inverse-sentence-frequency mass damped by
`log(len_i) + log(len_j)`. Ten speaker cues left the summary.

## Measures tested and REJECTED, with their numbers

These are negative results, not untried ideas.

- **Positional spread of a sentence's similarity partners** (the "apparatus is a locally self-similar clique"
  hypothesis). Mean normalized spread, apparatus vs body: Moby Dick 0.926 vs 0.960; Pride and Prejudice 1.006 vs
  0.921 -- *opposite direction*; albania, which has no apparatus at all, scores 0.758, lower than either. No
  separation.
- **Lexical distance from the document centroid, per sentence.** AUROC (apparatus ranked low): moby dick 0.387,
  pride and prejudice 0.286, jane eyre 0.294. Too weak, and not comparable across sources: albania's body scores
  0.0867 while Moby Dick's *apparatus* scores 0.0501 -- there is no threshold that is right for both.
- **Same, per contiguous block, with Otsu + MDL two-class-vs-one-class model selection** so a homogeneous document
  would not be split. MDL selected TWO CLASSES on all ten sources tested, including albania, alaska and albert
  einstein, which have no apparatus. It would have dropped 18/29 albania blocks, 20/102 jane eyre body blocks and
  36/98 little women body blocks. Rejected outright.
- **Adjacent lexical cohesion (TextTiling's signal).** Block-level AUROC: moby dick 0.318, pride and prejudice
  0.512, jane eyre 0.913 -- the direction reverses between sources. Useless.
- **Hapax share.** Block-level AUROC 1.000 on pride and prejudice and 0.990 on jane eyre, but 0.527 on moby dick
  (its Extracts are all about whales, so its apparatus vocabulary repeats). Not reliable alone, and albania's body
  scores 0.226, the same as Pride and Prejudice's apparatus.

## Wiring

`packages/kernel/src/production-turn-runtime.ts`, immediately after the contradiction fallback and before
`withCitation` -- the last point before `answer = spoken.text`, whose emptiness is what `packages/server/src/routes.ts:803`
turns into the 422.

Fires only when all four hold:

1. `!spoken.text.trim()` -- every narrower lane above (learned mouth, deterministic mouth, realizable-candidate
   fallback, contradiction fallback) has already produced nothing, so a narrower answer always wins.
2. authority is factual or reasoned, and the turn has admitted evidence.
3. the evidence is identity-bound: `evidenceIdentityBindsRequest`, the same authority retrieval admitted on.
4. every sentence of the summary is found verbatim inside one of the admitted spans -- otherwise nothing is said.

The spans that actually supplied the spoken sentences become `spoken.evidenceRefs`, so the existing `withCitation`
names the source exactly as it does for every other sourced answer. Traced as `mouth.source_summary_fallback` and
`mouth.source_summary_fallback.withheld`.

The decision itself is `summarizeAdmittedSource` in `source-summary.ts` -- pure, no clock, no storage -- so it is
unit-tested rather than only reachable through a live turn.

## Remaining uncertainty

- **An editorial preface is not separated.** When the only spans naming the source are the preface -- exactly the
  Pride and Prejudice identity window above -- the summary is the preface. Nothing I measured distinguishes a
  critical introduction from the work when the introduction quotes the work's own characters and is the majority of
  what is in view. The three lexical measures I tried are in the rejected list with their numbers. A corpus-level
  measure (apparatus is text a source shares with OTHER sources -- the Gutenberg licence is byte-identical across
  all 15 books here) would catch the licence but not a preface, and costs a corpus-wide query.
- **The identity window is a simulation.** The harness selects spans by literal title containment. The real runtime
  admits by source identity and then ranks, so its pool is broader and probably easier than this worst case. What
  the live turn actually admits for "tell me the plot of moby dick" has not been observed -- that needs the
  integrator's serialized `/api/turn`.
- **No live gate was run.** Everything here is offline. The wiring is gated behind four conditions and adds answers
  only where the turn previously returned 422, but that claim is argued from the code path, not measured live.
- **`splitSurfaceSentences` still ends a segment at every newline** for every other caller in the kernel. The
  unwrap lives inside `source-summary.ts` to bound the blast radius. If hard-wrapped sources reach other lanes
  (quotation recall, cloze selection), they see the same fragments this module used to see.
- The similarity change alters `centralSentences`'s scale. Nothing else in the repo called this module, so nothing
  else can have depended on the old values.
