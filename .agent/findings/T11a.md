# T11a — argument-role / predicate-frame correspondence

base: 3f0ac86 · harness: `tools/argument-frame-harness.mjs` · **NEGATIVE RESULT. No production change.**

## The question

Can an argument-role / predicate-frame signal be induced from this corpus, independent of orthography, that
separates find/found from mind/mound? **No.** Measured on the full corpus, not the hydration slice.

## The signal, and why it is not orthographic

Full corpus loaded once: **2,028 models, 317,571 types, 8,283,125 bigram records** streamed to a local triple
file, then scanned. Nothing about it reads the spelling of the pair.

1. **Frame vocabulary**, induced by `deriveClosedClassWords`'s own instrument: rank types by order-1 Kneser-Ney
   continuation count (how many DISTINCT histories a word continues), take the top K. Top of the induced ranking
   is `' and in - of to was as on for the is with at`. Production's limit is 96; the harness sweeps
   K = 48/96/192/384 so no single number decides the verdict.
2. **Frame profiles.** For a surface w, `L_w` = distribution over the immediately preceding word and `R_w` over
   the immediately following word, each restricted to the frame vocabulary and renormalised. Content words are
   dropped on purpose — T10 proved they are the part that does not transfer between related forms.
3. **The statistic.** `right = BC(R_u, R_v)`, `left = BC(L_u, L_v)` (Bhattacharyya coefficient, bounded in [0,1]),
   and **`S = right − left`**. The hypothesis in the brief predicts S is large and positive for an inflectional
   pair — same complement frame, different grammatical state — and near zero for two nouns.
4. **Scale.** 460–510 frequency-matched random pairs give S a background mean and sd, so "large" is measured.
   At K=96: background mean 0.0151, sd 0.1955.

Cost bounds, commented as such in the harness: 3,000 frequency-ranked types tracked for continuation sets,
8,000 types entering both-ends alignment, per-type predecessor set capped at 60,000.

## Three reasons this is a real negative, not a tuning failure

1. **`mind/mound` is the second-highest scorer of the eleven** (+0.252), above three required MATCHes —
   bind/bound +0.149, grind/ground −0.001, wind/wound −0.052. The one pair the brief named as the thing to
   separate is exactly where the signal fails. The gap find/found − mind/mound is 0.128, **0.65 background sd**.
2. **The top score in the entire diagnostic set is a required REFUSE**, `capita/capital` at +0.555, z +2.76.
   Inspect its components: `left` is 0.061 because `capita` has 6 frame tokens to the left — 95% of its tokens sit
   behind one word. So S is not reading argument role at all here; it is re-reading T6's boundness with the sign
   flipped, and a fixed-phrase fragment maximises it.
3. **9 of the 11 members are too sparse to measure.** Requiring >=50 frame tokens in *every* slot leaves exactly
   two members — find/found and wind/wound — and both are true, so the restricted set has no negatives and no
   AUROC is defined. `rewind`, `unwind`, `pind` have 0 frame tokens on one side; `grind` has 7; `mound` 42/50.
   The full corpus does not contain enough of these surfaces to decide the family however good the statistic is.
   This is the same wall T10 hit at the hydration budget, one order of magnitude further out.

## What I could not measure and why

- **`kound`** does not exist in 317,571 types, so `kind/kound` is undecidable by any corpus mechanism at any budget.
- **Trigram frames.** Only bigram records were streamed; `evidence_spans.features` carries `tri:a|b|c` but the
  per-surface trigram profile needs a different scan and the budget went on getting the bigram answer right.
  A two-sided frame (prev, next) as a joint cell is the obvious next thing and is untested.
- **Whether S has construct validity as a state detector at all.** The held-out family is consistent with it but
  not significantly so; a proper test needs a population of pairs with known state alternation, which requires the
  thing being built.

## Reproduce

```
node --max-old-space-size=7168 tools/argument-frame-harness.mjs --cache=<dir> [--heldout-rank=3] [--frame=96]
```
First run streams the full corpus read-only into `<dir>/bigrams.i32` (~100MB, about 6 minutes); later runs are local.
