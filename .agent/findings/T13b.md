## Safety-bearing thresholds

These decide whether a claim may be spoken, or what a reader is told about how far to trust it.

- `proof-calculus.ts` epistemic force ladder: `calculus.force_unknown_contradiction_floor`,
  `force_unknown_leakage_floor`, `force_proved_support_floor`, `force_proved_lcb_floor`,
  `force_observed_support_floor`, `force_observed_lcb_floor`, `force_inferred_support_floor`,
  `force_conjectured_support_floor`. These are the proved/observed/inferred/conjectured/invented labels.
- `semantic-proof-system.ts` `verdictFrom`: `proof.verdict_contradiction_floor`,
  `verdict_contradiction_dominance_ratio`, `verdict_entailed_{support,coverage,faithfulness}_floor`,
  `verdict_partial_{support,coverage}_floor`. Also `certifyingUnification`: `proof.certifying_role_floor`,
  `certifying_constraint_floor`, `certifying_contradiction_ceiling` -- these gate a certified fact.
- `semantic-obligations.ts` `entailmentVerdict`: `obligations.verdict_contradiction_floor`,
  `verdict_item_contradiction_floor`, and the five-way conjunction
  `entailment_{role_coverage,structural_coverage,relation_compatibility,transformation_support,faithfulness_lcb,stability}_floor`.
  Lowering any one of the conjunction speaks weaker claims. Discharge floors
  `transform_satisfied_support_floor` and `role_satisfied_fit_floor` are also safety-bearing.
- `translation.ts` force ladders: `translation.direct_preservation_floor`, `approximate_preservation_floor`,
  `aggregate_direct_share_floor`, `aggregate_direct_preservation_floor`, `aggregate_approximate_preservation_floor`.
  Direct means "carries the source meaning"; gloss and unknown mark a rendering the turn must not assert.
- `tool-cognition.ts` approval: `approval_risk_ceiling`, `approval_privacy_pressure_ceiling`,
  `operator_grant_risk_ceiling`, `operator_grant_eligibility_risk_ceiling`. These decide whether a capability runs
  without a human, and the last two cap what a standing grant can authorize.

## Values I believe are wrong (not changed)

- `semantic-obligations.ts` `entailmentVerdict` uses 0.42 for aggregate contradiction while
  `semantic-proof-system.ts` `verdictFrom` uses 0.55 for the same question on the same scale, and
  `proof-calculus.ts` `forceFrom` uses 0.45. Three different refutation bounds decide overlapping things; at most
  one of them can be right.
- `obligations.underdetermined_causal_mass_floor` is 0.05. Any trace of causal mass keeps a verdict out of
  "unknown"; it reads like a disabled condition rather than a chosen bound.
- `proof.certifying_role_floor` (0.45) is below `proof.verdict_partial_support_floor` (0.42)'s neighbourhood while
  `certifying_constraint_floor` is 0.68: a unification can certify a fact on weaker role agreement than the proof
  needs to call itself partial.
